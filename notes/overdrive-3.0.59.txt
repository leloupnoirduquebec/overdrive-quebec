OVERDRIVE QUÉBEC 3.0.59 — NOTES DE MISE À JOUR

Pour l'installer, jeu fermé : dans le lanceur, clique sur « Mise à jour disponible ». Seuls 2 modules changent : Camions et Base (environ 125 Mo). Cette version va avec American Truck Simulator 1.62. Tes sauvegardes se chargent comme avant.

En bref : des correctifs pour la remorqueuse de la bêta privée en cours. Sur la route, rien ne change pour les autres joueurs.

ENGLISH

Install, game closed: in the launcher, click « Mise à jour disponible » (update available). Only 2 modules change: Camions and Base (about 125 MB). This version goes with American Truck Simulator 1.62. Your saves load as before.

Fixes and adjustments
- Private beta, wrecker (only with the testers' beta access):
  - artwork paint jobs now carry on over our parts: the sleeper doors, the second front axle's fender, the plates and the whole rear body (before, these parts showed a single colour with every paint job);
  - on the body, the design picks up the rear of the sleeper's design at the same height, so it follows from the front;
  - 9 patterned paint jobs keep a plain body, in the colour of their plain parts: Edge Tracer, Juggernaut, Big Rocky, Leopard Tracks, Bareback Blanket, Touramerica, Connection Point, Crystal Dream and Winter Blast;
  - the overdrivequebec.ca lettering and the slogan have a wider black outline: they read on light paint jobs too;
  - other adjustments for the testers.

What does not change
- Pay, engines and gearboxes, headlights, the train horn, the trailers on sale and their load, cameras, the seat, suspension, brakes, sky, GPS, posters, traffic lights, trains and road traffic: the same as in 3.0.58.

Drive safe!

Mise à jour : ferme le jeu ET le lanceur, rouvre le lanceur (il installe la mise à jour), puis relance le jeu avant de te reconnecter au serveur.
Update: close the game AND the launcher, reopen the launcher (it installs the update), then start the game again before you reconnect to the server.
