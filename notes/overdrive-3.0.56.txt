OVERDRIVE QUÉBEC 3.0.56 — NOTES DE MISE À JOUR

Pour l'installer, jeu fermé : dans le lanceur, clique sur « Mise à jour disponible ». Seuls 2 modules changent : Camions et Base (environ 125 Mo). Cette version va avec American Truck Simulator 1.62. Tes sauvegardes se chargent comme avant.

En bref : des correctifs et du nouveau pour la bêta privée en cours. Sur la route, rien ne change pour les autres joueurs.

ENGLISH

Install, game closed: in the launcher, click « Mise à jour disponible » (update available). Only 2 modules change: Camions and Base (about 125 MB). This version goes with American Truck Simulator 1.62. Your saves load as before.

New
- Private beta: new things for the testers to discover (only with their beta access). Without that access, nothing changes in your game.

Fixes
- Private beta, wrecker (only with the testers' beta access):
  - the holes and gaps around the second front axle's fender and the side plate are closed;
  - the side lamps are straightened, flat on their plate;
  - the front wheel no longer lifts when the wrecker pulls a heavy load;
  - the tow job is offered more often in the freight market, and it now has its icon;
  - the paint chosen at the garage no longer spills onto the accessories.

What does not change
- Pay, engines, gearboxes, headlights, the train horn, the trailers on sale and their load, cameras, the seat, suspension, brakes, sky, GPS, posters, traffic lights, trains and road traffic: the same as in 3.0.55.

Drive safe!

Mise à jour : ferme le jeu ET le lanceur, rouvre le lanceur (il installe la mise à jour), puis relance le jeu avant de te reconnecter au serveur.
Update: close the game AND the launcher, reopen the launcher (it installs the update), then start the game again before you reconnect to the server.
