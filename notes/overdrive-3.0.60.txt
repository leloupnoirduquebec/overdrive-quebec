OVERDRIVE QUÉBEC 3.0.60 — NOTES DE MISE À JOUR

Pour l'installer, jeu fermé : dans le lanceur, clique sur « Mise à jour disponible ». Seuls 2 modules changent : Camions et Base (environ 130 Mo). Cette version va avec American Truck Simulator 1.62. Tes sauvegardes se chargent comme avant.

En bref : une petite surprise chez un concessionnaire, et des correctifs pour la remorqueuse de la bêta privée en cours.

ENGLISH

Install, game closed: in the launcher, click « Mise à jour disponible » (update available). Only 2 modules change: Camions and Base (about 130 MB). This version goes with American Truck Simulator 1.62. Your saves load as before.

New
- A little surprise has slipped into a dealer… Keep your eyes open: it is yours to find.

Fixes and adjustments
- Private beta, wrecker (only with the testers' beta access):
  - when it tows a wrecked truck, the wheel-lift kit (the forks under the wheels and the straps over them) now stays visible once coupled, while driving and from afar (before, it showed while coupling, then vanished);
  - the second front axle's fender is closed on the inside: you no longer see through it;
  - other adjustments for the testers.

What does not change
- Pay, engines and gearboxes, headlights, the train horn, cameras, the seat, suspension, brakes, sky, GPS, posters, traffic lights, trains and road traffic: the same as in 3.0.59.

Drive safe!

Mise à jour : ferme le jeu ET le lanceur, rouvre le lanceur (il installe la mise à jour), puis relance le jeu avant de te reconnecter au serveur.
Update: close the game AND the launcher, reopen the launcher (it installs the update), then start the game again before you reconnect to the server.
